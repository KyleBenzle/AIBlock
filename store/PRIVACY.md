# Slop Blocker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

Slop Blocker is a browser extension that hides AI-generated content on webpages.

**What it collects:** Nothing. Slop Blocker has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no developer-operated servers.

**What stays on your device:** Your settings, block statistics, a short history of recently blocked items (with a snippet of up to 100 characters, so you can review decisions), and caches. All of this is stored locally in your browser through Chrome's storage, and you can clear it from the settings page at any time.

**Image metadata checks:** To read AI-provenance metadata, the extension may re-download the beginning of an image (up to 512 KB) that the page you are viewing has already loaded. The request goes directly to that image's own server, and no cookies are sent with it. No data is sent to the developer.

**Optional AI-assisted detection (off by default):** If you turn this on and give consent, the extension sends passages of visible webpage text to the AI provider *you* configure (OpenAI, xAI, an OpenAI-compatible service, or a local Ollama server), using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy then applies to that text. Text from forms, editable fields, and mail/chat/document websites is never sent. Your API key is stored only in your browser and is sent only to the provider you selected.

**Sharing:** The developer never receives, sells, or shares your data.
